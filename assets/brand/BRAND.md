# Brand — Roseford

- **Motif:** the heroine before the seal — Isolde, from behind, in white-and-rose
  keeper's robes, facing a colossal sealed dungeon door whose centre is a
  glowing rose seal ringed with ward circles.
- **Palette:** amber candlelight and rose-red on near-black stone; series frame: dark background, hot pink
  (#ff2e88) accents — the NTRanch book theme.
- `header.png` — hero/header art (itch cover + repo header), 2736×912.
- `icon.png` — square icon derived from the hero (centre crop on the rose seal, 512×512).

**Deviation from the sibling pattern, recorded:** the other jam entries'
brand art is motif-only (no characters, D-292). This entry's brand art
carries its heroine because the stand-up commission (D-1123) specified her
— a resurrected shrine maiden before a sealed dungeon door — as the art.
The art is original AI-generated work for this title (generation record:
`media-generation-header-1794210040.json` beside this file); the glyph
rings on the door are carved ward ornament, not text. D-292 still governs
all *in-game* art for this repo: keeper art only, stand-ins logged, no
generation jobs.
