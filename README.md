# Roseford

**Nice Thoughts Ranch** · Torogao Jam Program entry 15 (Wave E — Systems, adopted D-1130) · Isolde · **Rust**

A resurrected shrine maiden descends a sealed dungeon whose wards tune her — corruption is her power curve, her memory, and her price.

An 18+ game built for the weekly Gameotron jam on itch.io (NTRanch), as one
rung of the Torogao system-validation program (D-333/D-334/D-335/D-336),
added as its fifteenth entry by Owner ruling D-1123 (2026-11-08). The full
design lives in [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md); the planning book lives in
[`book/`](book/) (mdBook, NTRanch theme); this repo's bloch roadmap is
[`.ai/roadmap.md`](.ai/roadmap.md).

- **Delivery:** one self-contained `index.html` (see `docs` in the book).
- **Cast/art law (D-292):** existing keeper art only; a missing asset is an
  abstract stand-in, logged — never a generation job. (Brand art is the one
  commissioned exception, D-1123; see `assets/brand/BRAND.md`.)
- **Quality bar:** Laplace3D-class animation or the medium's equivalent
  (book chapter: Quality Bar).
- **Audio:** nothing is ever played aloud by an agent; the Owner presses play.

Status: scaffold (design + baseline config). No playable build exists yet —
the build is a later rung, gated on this design and the week's preflight.
The jam clock for this entry starts on the Owner's word for week 15.
