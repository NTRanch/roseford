# Roseford

**Entry 15 · Wave E — Systems (proposed) · Isolde · Rust**

A resurrected shrine maiden descends a sealed dungeon whose wards tune her — corruption is her power curve, her memory, and her price.

One entry of the Torogao Jam Program: weekly NSFW games that validate
Torogao's systems in mechanism groups, cycling the estate's languages,
each closing with a seahawk playtest, a breakage log, and a port-back
note with a language verdict. Roseford is the programme's fifteenth
entry, added by Owner ruling D-1123 (2026-11-08) after the original
fourteen closed, to validate a persistent state system at RPG scale.
The full design of record is `DESIGN.md` in the repo root.
